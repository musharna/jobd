- **A finished job no longer displays a queue warning that resolved when it started running.** `warning` is one column serving two purposes — durable explanations of a terminal state (`parent_failed:`, `auto-preempted in favor of`) and transient advisories about the queue a job is sitting in (`will queue behind job N`, `blocked:`, `no matching worker —`, the submit-time projects.yaml notice). The sweeper only clears the second kind on rows that are still QUEUED, so a job that left the queue kept its advisory for the life of the row: `job list` was rendering `⚠ will queue behind job 3037 on laptop` under a job that had completed eleven hours earlier, in the present tense. Read surfaces now suppress queue advisories once a job is terminal, while keeping the warnings that explain _why_ it is cancelled or preempted. Applied in `_to_info`, where `GET /jobs`, `GET /jobs/{id}`, the submit/cancel/preempt responses and the MCP tools all converge — previously `job status` omitted the warning while `job list` showed it, so the two surfaces disagreed about the same row. The stored value is untouched; only the presentation stops asserting a resolved prediction as current.
